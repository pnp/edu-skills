---
name: slide-master-compliance
description: "Use when I ask Copilot in PowerPoint to review a presentation for Slide Master compliance, find slides using off-theme colors, fonts, backgrounds, or manual layouts, recommend the correct master layout, or move slides onto approved theme and layout styles. Triggers include 'check the slide master,' 'audit this deck's theme,' 'fix off-brand slides,' and 'use the right layout.'"
---

Review the open presentation for compliance with its existing Slide Master and theme. Do not create a new master, theme, or layout.

1. Inspect the presentation's masters, layouts, theme colors, theme fonts, placeholders, backgrounds, and slide dimensions.
2. Audit every slide. Identify direct formatting, custom colors or fonts, manually positioned content that duplicates a layout, missing or altered placeholders, and slides assigned to an unsuitable layout.
3. Treat intentional exceptions such as screenshots, product UI, partner logos, data visualizations, and approved brand assets separately. Do not recolor or distort them merely to match the theme.
4. Report: overall compliance (Compliant, Minor fixes, or Needs remediation); approved masters and layouts found; issues by slide; and the exact existing layout recommended for each affected slide. Explain why each change is needed.
5. When asked to fix the deck, first confirm the proposed slide-to-layout mapping. Then apply the existing layout, move content into its placeholders, reset theme-controlled formatting, and preserve content, meaning, reading order, speaker notes, links, and animations where possible.
6. After editing, re-audit the changed slides and summarize what changed, what remains intentionally custom, and anything requiring manual review.
