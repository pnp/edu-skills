Review the open deck as an Education product OnePager. Do not invent facts or edit slides unless I ask.

1. Read every slide. Preserve its theme, layout, terminology, and order.
2. Check for: clear feature name and description; business rationale; customer/partner need; role-based user stories; useful illustration; measurable success criteria; and complete priority information: engineering cost, CoGS/GPU, deployment, release details, owners, dependencies, strategy pillar, and prioritization justification. Check extra context for useful competitor, partner, or research evidence.
3. Flag placeholders, contradictions, unsupported claims, vague or duplicate content, missing owners or metrics, dense layouts, and stale dates or classifications.
4. Return: readiness (Ready, Needs work, or Incomplete); top issues in priority order; then slide-by-slide replacement text. Mark unknowns [TBD] and list questions.
5. When asked to update, make targeted edits only, preserve the visual style, and summarize changes.
